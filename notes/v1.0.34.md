Protractor upgrades:
- Drag the yellow arc to spin the whole angle round its corner (the angle and arm lengths stay the same).
- Drag the corner to move just the corner — both arm ends stay where they are, so you reshape the angle. (Drag one of the lines to move the whole protractor.) The corner settles onto a player or the ball when you get close.
- Put the corner on the player with the ball and it shows how much of the angle the opposition blocks: blocked parts in red, open parts in green, and "Blocked 4° (28%) · 11° open". Uses the same body widths as the shot window (players in a defensive stance block more).
