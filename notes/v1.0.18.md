The whole app now works with a controller:
- Left stick moves a crosshair cursor that slows over buttons, menus and players and locks onto them when you let go. Hold the stick all the way for fast travel.
- A clicks (hold to drag players or draw lines), B goes back, X opens the right-click menu, Y undoes.
- LB / RB change tool, hold LT for aerial/lofted, RT for Extra Effort while drawing a run.
- D-pad steps and zooms, Menu plays/pauses, right stick pans, scrolls panels or looks around in over-the-shoulder, L3 cycles follow, R3 fits the board.
- Drop-down menus cycle with A, sliders follow the cursor while A is held.
- Press View / Share for the full button map. Moving the mouse hides the controller cursor.
- Fixed the hint bar showing "undefined" for the Shot tool.
