Controller support (Xbox, PlayStation and most USB pads):
- A / Cross or Start: play / pause. B / Circle: stop.
- D-pad left / right: previous / next step. D-pad up / down: first / last step.
- LB / RB: follow previous / next (ball, then the 10 players).
- LT / RT: playback slower / faster.
- Y / Triangle: over-the-shoulder on/off. X / Square: focus on/off.
- Right stick: look around in over-the-shoulder (R3 re-centres); otherwise up/down zooms.
- Left stick: pan the board.
