- **Hide lines** (button next to the eye, or Shift+H): hides every arrow, pass, shot, run, wall pass, keeper jump and pen mark to show just the positions. Zones and text stay. Works in playback and GIF/PNG exports too.
- **Ruler** upgrades: it stays on the board after you draw it. Drag an end to swing it around the other end at the same length (Shift-drag an end to stretch it), drag the middle to move it somewhere else as it is. Shift while drawing snaps to 15°, and the label now shows its angle too. Esc clears it.
- New **Protractor** (Shift+U): drag the first arm from the corner, then move and click to place the second — shows the angle and both arm lengths. Drag the corner to move it, an arm end to swing it (Shift to stretch).
