Curved shots, from in-game measurements (full curve on a full-power shot lands ~4 units off the aim after 33 units):
- Ground and aerial shots can curve left or right. Draw the shot to where it should end up, then bend it: scroll the mouse wheel or press [ / ] while drawing, or right-click a shot and use the Curve slider. On a controller, push the right stick left/right while drawing.
- A dashed line and crosshair mark show where to aim in game to get that curve.
- Playback, 3D views and over-the-shoulder follow the curve.
- Selected shots now say "Shot selected".
