New: **Export GIF** (Share panel). Records the whole play from step 1 as an animated GIF, in whatever view you're in — flat, 3D or over-the-shoulder — at your playback speed.
- Choose the size (480 / 720 / 960 px wide) and smoothness (10 / 20 / 25 fps).
- Options: pause between steps, a step bar with the step number and coaching note, a small Wing-Board tag, loop forever.
- Recorded frame-by-frame on a virtual clock, so it's perfectly smooth on any PC and usually takes only a few seconds. Files are small (a 3-step play at 720 px / 20 fps is about 250 KB), and it warns you if one is over Discord's 10 MB limit.
