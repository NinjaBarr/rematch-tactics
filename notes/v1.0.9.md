- Aerial wall passes and wall shots: right-click > Wall contact height. The range comes from ball physics: shot speed 33.4 units/s with a 60-unit max range, passes/lobs at tap speed with a 55-unit range, aim capped at 45°. The wall shows how high it hits (e.g. "4.2 m UP"), the 3D arc follows it, and higher lofts take longer to arrive.
- Wall passes can be switched to wall shots (right-click > Shot).
- Aerial shots: press Shift while drawing a shot (or right-click > Aerial). They draw as a solid arc, like aerial passes but in the shot style.
- The separate Aerial pass tool is gone: press Shift while drawing a pass or shot to loft it.
- Follow hotkeys: ` (~) follows the ball, 1–5 the home players, 6–9 and 0 the away players; press again to stop. Drawing colours moved to Shift+1–5 and fit-the-pitch to Shift+0.
