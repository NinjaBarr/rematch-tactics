New **Options** window (gear button in the top bar, or Ctrl+,):
- General: what opens on launch, show/hide the tutorial play, hotkey hints, name tags, style picker, version and Check for updates, and Reset all options (your plays aren't touched).
- Sound: master volume, kick sounds on/off, UI sound set and its own volume.
- Playback & camera: player moves, default step length for Timed plays, over-the-shoulder look speed and invert up/down.
- Controller: cursor speed, lock-on strength (off / light / normal / strong) and the button map.
- Shortcuts: every keyboard shortcut in one list.
The step bar and Teams panel are tidier now that these live in Options.
The controller crosshair now matches your style and colours: its colour follows your accent, and its shape changes with the board style (ring, brackets, diamond, chalk, marker or bubble).
