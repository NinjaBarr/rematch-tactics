New: **Timed steps** (tick "Timed" in the step bar).
- Every step lasts a set time — type it in the box next to Timed (1.0 s to start). Each step keeps its own length and new steps copy the current one.
- Everything moves at real game speed. Whatever doesn't fit in a step carries on into the next one: the part that doesn't fit is dimmed, with a gold marker showing how late it lands ("+0.6 s → next step", or "add a step" on the last one).
- Long balls, long runs and combos keep working across steps — a pass still meets its runner, the receiver waits for a ball that's still in the air.
- A running clock shows during playback, and the step buttons show when each step starts.
- Reach rings (the ring button next to the time): how far every player can sprint in the step, Extra Effort reach for the player you point at, and pass / full-power shot reach from the ball.
- Turning Timed off gives you the old behaviour back; your steps aren't changed.
