REMATCH Tactics - put it online so anyone (iPhone, Android, PC) can use it
==========================================================================

This folder is a complete website. Once it's online you get a normal web
address to send people. On iPhone they open it in Safari and tap
Share > Add to Home Screen, and it becomes an app with its own icon that
opens full screen and works offline.

Easiest: Netlify Drop (free, about 2 minutes, no coding)
  1. Unzip rematch-tactics-web.zip so you have a folder with index.html in it.
  2. On a computer, go to  https://app.netlify.com/drop
  3. Drag that whole folder onto the page.
  4. Sign up / log in (free) to claim the site. Until you claim it, Netlify keeps
     it behind a temporary password, so claim it before sending the link out.
  5. You get an address like https://something-random.netlify.app
     In the project settings you can change the name, e.g. rematch-tactics.netlify.app
  To update later: open the project in Netlify > Production deploys > drag the
  new folder into the drop area.
  The address stays the same, and people's phones pick up the new version.

Alternative: GitHub Pages (free, good if you already use GitHub)
  1. New repository (public), e.g. rematch-tactics.
  2. Add file > Upload files > drag everything inside this folder in > Commit.
  3. Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch > main / (root) > Save.
  4. A minute later it's at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/rematch-tactics/

What to tell your friends (iPhone)
  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open "Tactics" from the home screen. It runs full screen and offline.
  Android: open the link in Chrome > menu > "Add to Home screen" / "Install app".

Co-op (planning together)
  Top bar > Co-op > type your name > Host a session. Send the 6-letter room
  code (or "Copy invite link" on the website) to the others; they open Co-op,
  type their name and the code, and press Join. Everyone sees the same board,
  each other's cursors, and edits live. As host you can switch off
  "Everyone can edit" (others watch) and switch on "Everyone follows me"
  (your step changes and playback show on everyone's screen).

Good to know
  - Each person's plays are saved on their own device (nothing is uploaded).
    On a computer in Chrome or Edge, Plays > "Use a folder" keeps them as files
    in a folder you choose, with its subfolders as categories.
  - To send someone a play: Share > Export play file; they use Import.
  - On a phone, Export PNG opens the share sheet (Save Image puts it in Photos).
