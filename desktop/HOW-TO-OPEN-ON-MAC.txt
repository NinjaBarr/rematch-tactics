REMATCH Tactics for Mac
=======================

1. Unzip, then drag "REMATCH Tactics.app" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because
   it isn't from the App Store or a paid Apple developer account.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to REMATCH Tactics. Confirm once more.
   (On older macOS: right-click the app > Open > Open.)
After that it opens normally every time. Drag it to the Dock to keep it there.

Which download?  Apple menu > About This Mac:
  "Chip: Apple M1/M2/M3/M4..."  -> REMATCH Tactics-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip
  "Processor: Intel..."          -> REMATCH Tactics-Mac-Intel.zip

Your plays are files in Documents > REMATCH Tactics Plays. Make folders in
there (in Finder or with "+ Folder" in the app) to sort them; the app shows
them as categories. Plays > Change picks a different folder (iCloud Drive,
Dropbox...) if you want them synced between computers.
